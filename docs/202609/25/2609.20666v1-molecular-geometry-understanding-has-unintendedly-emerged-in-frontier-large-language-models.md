## 摘要
大语言模型（LLM）已在解决以自然语言表述的复杂化学问题方面展现出强大能力。然而，化学家执行的许多任务需要理解化合物的三维结构并对其进行推理——据我们所知，这些能力既非 LLM 开发者有意设计，也未在当代模型中接受过测试。这种理解对于自主分子发现与药物研发至关重要，而后者是 AI 应用于化学领域的圣杯之一。我们在现代 LLM 中测试了这一能力，测试对象为 27 个小有机分子的 810 个几何构型及其 DFT 能量。引人注目的是，尽管 2025 年 7 月之前发布的模型难以按稳定性对构象异构体进行排序，但许多近期前沿模型，包括 GPT-5.6 Sol、Kimi K3 和 Gemini 3.6 Flash，达到了有竞争力的准确率，超过了 Universal Force Field，而 GPT-6 Astra 和 Claude Opus 5 则非常接近现代 GFN-FF 力场。对模型排序解释的分析表明，对于具有明确定义的分子内相互作用——氢键——的分子，它们表现最佳，因为氢键在科学语言中得到了很好的量化；与此同时，除两个最新模型——GPT-6 Astra 和 Claude Opus 5——之外，所有模型都难以处理由定义松散的概念（如环张力）主导的分子，而力场在这些分子上表现出色，这表明科学语言本身可能对 LLM 施加了约束。值得注意的是，模型对构象异构体排序的能力与其在科学、编程和抽象推理基准上的表现密切相关，这表明该能力是在模型训练于相互关联但概念上不同的任务时意外涌现的。得益于这种将单纯的科学知识泛化为对分子结构的理解，当前前沿模型为 AI 驱动的分子设计提供了现实的起点。

## Abstract
Large language models (LLMs) have already shown strong capabilities in solving complex chemical problems expressed in natural language. Yet, many tasks performed by chemists require understanding of 3D structures of chemical compounds and reasoning about them -- abilities, which, to the best of our knowledge, were neither intended by LLM developers nor tested in contemporary models. Such understanding is essential for autonomous molecular discovery and drug development, which is one of the Holy Grails of AI application to chemistry. We tested this capability in modern LLMs on 810 geometries and DFT energies of 27 small organic molecules. Strikingly, while models released before July 2025 struggled to rank conformers by stability, many recent frontier models, including GPT-5.6 Sol, Kimi K3, and Gemini 3.6 Flash achieved competitive accuracy, outperforming the Universal Force Field, and GPT-6 Astra and Claude Opus 5 came very close to a modern GFN-FF force field. Analysis of models' explanations for their rankings suggests that they perform best for molecules with well-defined intramolecular interactions -- hydrogen bonds -- which are well quantified in scientific language; at the same time all models except the two newest -- GPT-6 Astra and Claude Opus 5 -- struggle with molecules governed by loosely defined concepts (e.g., ring strain), where force fields excel, suggesting that the scientific language itself might impose constraints on LLMs. Notably, a model's ability to rank conformers is strongly associated with its performance on scientific, coding, and abstract-reasoning benchmarks, suggesting that it emerged unintendedly from models training on linked but conceptually different tasks. Thanks to this generalization of mere scientific knowledge into understanding molecular structures, current frontier models offer a realistic starting point for AI-driven molecular design.
