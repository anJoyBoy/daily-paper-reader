## 摘要
采用最大能量耗散原理，以距全局平衡的距离变量（广义位移）来表述非线性化学热力学，并应用最优控制解释以发展变分公式。选择类代价泛函以支持这种公式对应于最大能量耗散原理的提议。利用该方法，提出了非线性化学热力学的变分框架，包括一般合作动力学模型。该公式与标准线性非平衡化学热力学吻合良好。

## Abstract
The maximum energy dissipation principle is employed to nonlinear chemical thermodynamics in terms of distance variable (generalized displacement) from the global equilibrium, applying the optimal control interpretation to develop a variational formulation. The cost-like functional was chosen to support the suggestion that such a formulation corresponds to the maximum energy dissipation principle. Using this approach, the variational framework was proposed for a nonlinear chemical thermodynamics, including general cooperative kinetics model. The formulation is in good agreement with standard linear non-equilibrium chemical thermodynamics.
